Layouts

The keyboard follows the system layout, however you switch it. Its own EN and RU keys send Caps Lock and Shift + Caps Lock.

In Russian, holding Symbols switches the system to English for as long as you hold it, so every symbol comes out the same in both layouts.

Symbol rolls

Roll inward on the left hand, pinky towards index:

( )[ ]< >->=><-!=<=|>~/!(

Slide one finger along its column:

/**/#!

Braces and quotes are on the right hand, so ${ ={ (" alternate hands.

Staying on a layer

While holding a layer key, tap Lock (bottom right) and let go: the layer stays on. Tap Lock again to leave. Works for every layer you hold.

Right display

EN or RU in large type, the name of the layer you hold, and along the bottom the modifiers you hold, lit in finger order from pinky to index.

Home row

Each home key is a letter when tapped and a modifier when held: Control, Option, Command, Shift from pinky to index, mirrored on the right hand. On Linux, Control and the Super key trade places, so Control sits under the middle finger there.

A modifier only takes hold with a key from the other hand, or after a short pause, so fast typing never triggers one by accident.

Reading the keys

  • a⌃Tap for the letter, hold for the lower edge
  • tHome position, where the fingers rest
  • 7What the layer is for
  • +Extras on the layer
  • heldThe key you are holding
  • TabSame as the base layer
  • Does nothing
